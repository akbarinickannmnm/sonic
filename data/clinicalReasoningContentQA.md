# SONIC Clinical Reasoning Content QA

Each of the 40 cases contains:
- 40 fixed History answers (CR-H01 to CR-H40)
- 12 fixed Physical Exam answers (CR-P01 to CR-P12)
- 25 fixed Investigation results (CR-I01 to CR-I25)

The investigation bank intentionally uses CR-I25 as a targeted diagnostic-test/imaging slot so disease-specific confirmatory studies can be represented without changing the fixed bank size.

Educational note: all values are synthetic teaching cases, not patient data. Clinical use requires faculty review and adaptation to local protocols.
